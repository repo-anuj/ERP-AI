### Phase 1: Payroll System (Critical for Manager Side)
- **Salary Computation Engine**
  - Implement Indian tax computations (TDS, Professional Tax)
  - Calculate statutory deductions PF, ESI, etc.
- **Payslip Generation**
  - Automated payslip creation and storage for employees
- **Bank File Generation**
  - Prepare bank transfer files compliant with Indian banking standards
- **Tax Filing and Compliance**
  - Automate Form 16 generation and related tax filings
- **Overtime & Variable Pay Calculations**

### Phase 2: Advanced Analytics and Reporting
- **Customizable HR Reports**
  - Employee attrition, attendance trends, leave utilization, recruitment analytics
- **Predictive Analytics**
  - Attrition prediction models specific to Indian HR patterns
- **Audit and Compliance Reports**
  - Reports aligning with Indian labor laws and audit requirements
- **KPI Tracking per Department/Project**

### Phase 3: Automated HR Workflows
- **Onboarding Workflow**
  - Task lists and progress tracking for new hires
- **Performance Review Cycles**
  - Automated scheduling, reminders, and progress tracking
- **Offboarding Workflow**
  - Exit clearance tasks, asset recovery, exit interviews
- **Leave Approval Automation**
  - Multi-level approval workflow refinement

### Phase 4: Access Control and Security
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**
  - Granular permissions for managers, admins, HR staff
- **Data Security Enhancements**
  - Encryption of sensitive data, audit trails for access

### Manager Dashboard Components Requiring Updates/Creation

1. **Employee Management Dashboard**
   - View employee summaries with payroll and attendance insights
   - Leave requests dashboard integrated with approval workflows

2. **Payroll Dashboard**
   - Salary overview, tax deduction summaries, payslip management
   - Alerts for statutory compliance (PF/ESI due dates)

3. **Department Management Dashboard**
   - Department-wise performance, headcount, and KPIs

4. **HR Analytics Dashboard**
   - Attrition rates, attendance patterns, leave trends customized for Indian context

5. **Task and Workflow Dashboard**
   - Onboarding, performance review, offboarding tasks and alerts
